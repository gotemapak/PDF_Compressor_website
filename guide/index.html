How to Compress a PDF in Chrome Without Uploading Your Files

A Chrome extension lets you shrink a PDF's file size by up to 90% entirely on your own device - no upload, no account, no internet connection required after install. Here's exactly how it works.

Install Extension

The 4 Steps

1

Install the Extension

Add PDF Compressor to Chrome from the Chrome Web Store. No sign-up is required to use it.

2

Drag and Drop Your PDF

Open the extension and drop in the PDF file you want to shrink, or select it from your files. Files up to 150MB are supported.

3

Pick a Compression Level

Choose from multiple quality levels - from high quality to extreme compression - depending on how small you need the file to be.

4

Download the Result

Download your compressed PDF. Everything happens locally in your browser, so the file never left your computer.

Why Local (Offline) Processing Matters

Most PDF compressors on the web work the same way: you upload your file to a server, the server compresses it, and you download the result. That means your document - which might contain contracts, IDs, financial statements, or anything else you'd rather not send to a third-party server - briefly leaves your control.

PDF Compressor takes a different approach. All compression happens locally, directly in your Chrome browser. Your file is read from your computer, compressed on your computer, and saved back to your computer - it's never uploaded anywhere. That also means no sign-up or account is needed, and once the extension is installed, it keeps working even without an internet connection - useful for working on a plane, in an area with unreliable connectivity, or simply if you'd rather not depend on a server being available.

Built for Large Files

Many free online PDF compressors cap how large a file you can upload, often well below 100MB, and may limit how many files you can process per day or per month. PDF Compressor supports files up to 150MB each, with no monthly limits - so it's well suited to large documents and presentations as well as smaller files.
